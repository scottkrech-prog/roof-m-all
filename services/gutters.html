Direct answer

What homeowners should know about gutters.

Gutters protect the roof edge, fascia, siding, landscaping, and foundation by moving water where it belongs.

Gutter problems can show up as overflow, sagging runs, loose downspouts, fascia damage, stains, or water collecting near the foundation.

Roof-M-All serves Charlotte, Mint Hill, Matthews, Weddington, Waxhaw, Marvin, and nearby communities from its Mint Hill base.

When to call about gutters

  • Overflow during moderate rain
  • Sagging, pulling, or leaking gutter runs
  • Downspouts draining too close to the home
  • Fascia staining or soft wood
  • Gutter damage after wind, trees, or hail

FAQs

Gutters questions.

Does Roof-M-All install or replace gutters?

Yes. Gutters are listed as one of Roof-M-All’s exterior services.

Should gutters be addressed during roof replacement?

Often it is worth evaluating gutters during roof replacement because roof edges, fascia, drainage, and runoff all work together.

What affects gutter cost?

Linear footage, number of stories, downspouts, material, removal, fascia condition, access, and add-ons can all affect the final number.
