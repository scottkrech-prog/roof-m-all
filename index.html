FAQs · what homeowners ask us

Frequently asked questions.

Answers about inspections, storm damage, insurance, timelines, and cleanup.

Do I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the age of the roof, the size of the problem, and whether the damage is isolated or widespread. Roof-M-All starts with an inspection so you understand the practical options before making a decision.

Do you help with storm damage and insurance claims?

Yes. If wind or hail may be involved, Roof-M-All can inspect the damage, document what they find, and help you understand the next steps in the insurance process.

How long does a roof replacement usually take?

Many roof projects can be completed in 1 to 3 days (provided materials are ready and weather cooperates). The exact timeline depends on roof size, complexity, material choice, and any repairs needed.

Will you protect my property and clean up afterward?

Yes. The crew takes steps to protect the property during the work and clean up debris when the job is complete.

What areas do you serve?

Roof-M-All serves Charlotte, Mint Hill, Matthews, Weddington, Waxhaw, Marvin, and surrounding North Carolina and South Carolina communities.
