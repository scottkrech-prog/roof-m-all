## 5. Services Roof-M-All Handles

## Roof Repair / Leak Repair

### What to say
Roof-M-All can inspect roof leaks, missing shingles, lifted shingles, flashing problems, storm-related damage, and other roof issues. The first step is figuring out whether the problem is isolated enough for repair or whether replacement should be considered.

### Common customer questions
**Q: Do I need a repair or a full replacement?**  
A: It depends on the age of the roof, the amount of damage, and whether the issue is isolated or widespread. Roof-M-All starts with an inspection so you can compare practical options before deciding.

**Q: Can you fix a leak?**  
A: Roof-M-All can inspect leaks and roof problem areas. Some leaks can be repaired; others point to broader roof wear, flashing issues, storm damage, or a roof system near the end of its life.

**Q: What are signs I need roof repair?**  
A: Missing or lifted shingles, water stains inside, active dripping, damaged flashing, soft spots, granules in gutters, visible storm damage, or repeated leaks are all reasons to schedule an inspection.

### Ballpark pricing guidance
Use carefully. Based on publicly visible 2026 Charlotte/North Carolina cost guidance, roof leak repairs are often discussed in the low hundreds to low thousands. A reasonable safe script is:

> “Small roof repairs can sometimes be in the hundreds, while more involved leak or storm repairs can run into the low thousands. Public Charlotte-area cost guides often show roof leak repairs roughly around $360–$1,550, but every roof is different. Roof-M-All would need to inspect it before giving a real number.”

## Roof Replacement

### What to say
Roof-M-All handles roof replacement when a roof is too worn, damaged, storm-affected, or unreliable for another short-term repair. The team can explain material options, project timing, cleanup expectations, and the next steps.

### Common customer questions
**Q: How do I know if I need a new roof?**  
A: Warning signs include repeated leaks, missing or curled shingles, storm or hail damage, soft spots, visible aging, granule loss, and a roof nearing the end of its expected life. An inspection is the best way to know whether repair or replacement makes sense.

**Q: How long does roof replacement usually take?**  
A: Many roof projects can be completed in 1 to 3 days if materials are ready and weather cooperates. Larger or more complex roofs, decking repairs, specialty materials, and weather can extend the timeline.

**Q: Can I stay home during roof replacement?**  
A: In most cases, yes. Roofing work happens outside, but it can be loud and disruptive. Roof-M-All can explain what to expect before work begins.

### Ballpark pricing guidance
Use carefully. Public 2026 Charlotte-area sources commonly show asphalt shingle roof replacement in a broad range, often around $8,000–$16,000 for many average homes, or roughly $4–$11 per square foot depending on source, material, and roof complexity.

Safe script:

> “For an asphalt shingle roof replacement around Charlotte, many public 2026 cost guides show broad ballparks from about $8,000 to $16,000 for a typical home, with some roofs lower or much higher depending on size and complexity. Metal, specialty, steep, cut-up, or large roofs can cost substantially more. Roof-M-All would need to inspect and measure the roof to give a real estimate.”

Avoid saying “your roof will cost X.” Say “many projects fall somewhere around…” and immediately offer inspection.

## Storm Damage / Wind / Hail

### What to say
Roof-M-All can inspect for visible wind, hail, or storm-related roof damage, document what is found, and help homeowners understand the next step. The insurance company decides whether damage is covered.

### Common customer questions
**Q: We just had a storm. Should I have the roof checked?**  
A: Yes, especially if you see missing shingles, lifted shingles, dents, debris impact, leaks, granules in gutters, or neighbors are finding damage. Some storm damage is not obvious from the ground.

**Q: Do you help with insurance claims?**  
A: Roof-M-All can inspect the roof, document visible damage, and help you understand the insurance process. Your insurance company decides coverage and approval.

**Q: Should I call insurance first or Roof-M-All first?**  
A: Many homeowners prefer to have a roofing inspection first so they know whether visible damage is present before opening a claim. If there is active interior damage or an emergency, protect the home and call for help quickly.

**Q: Can storm damage be repaired instead of replacing the whole roof?**  
A: Sometimes. If damage is isolated, a repair may be enough. If damage is widespread or the roof is already near end-of-life, replacement may be the better long-term option.

### Escalation rule
If the visitor says there is active leaking, interior water, sagging ceiling, electrical concerns, or recent severe damage, the bot should say:

> “Because water is actively getting in, please call Roof-M-All at 704-520-9299 so the team can help you take the next step quickly. If there is any electrical danger or unsafe condition, stay away from that area and contact emergency help as appropriate.”

## 7. Pricing Knowledge: How the Bot Should Handle Ballpark Requests

People will ask “How much does a roof cost?” or “Can you give me a ballpark?” The bot should help without pretending to quote.

1. Acknowledge the question.
2. Say what range is common publicly / generally.
3. Explain why it varies.
4. Offer a free estimate/inspection.
5. Collect lead details.

### Universal pricing variables
- Roof size / square footage.
- Roof pitch and height.
- Number of roof layers to remove.
- Shingle/metal/rubber/flat material choice.
- Decking condition and hidden damage.
- Flashing, vents, skylights, chimneys, valleys, and complexity.
- Access and property protection needs.
- Permits/code requirements.
- Storm/insurance documentation needs.
- Warranty/product tier.
- Gutters, siding, fascia, or related exterior repairs.
