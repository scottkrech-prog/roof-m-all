What affects price?

Roofing

Roof size, pitch, layers, decking condition, shingle/metal system, flashing, vents, valleys, access, warranty/product tier, and storm documentation can all move the number.

Siding

Material, wall area, trim details, removal, hidden damage, window/door count, and access are the big variables.

Gutters

Linear footage, material, number of stories, downspouts, removal, fascia condition, and add-ons such as guard systems affect the range.
