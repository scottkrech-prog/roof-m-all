Accessibility Statement

Last updated: May 20, 2026

ADA Compliance Disclaimer

Roof-M-All strives to maintain a website that is accessible and consistent with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) as reasonably possible. We aim to improve accessibility as standards, technology, and website content evolve.

We recognize that usability may not function perfectly in every area of the website for every visitor, browser, device, or assistive technology. Our efforts are ongoing, and we continue to review and improve the website experience.

If You Need Assistance

If you have difficulty using this website, or with a particular web page or function on this site, please contact Roof-M-All by phone. We will make reasonable efforts to assist you and provide the information you need.

If you find an inaccessible area on the site, please specify the web page and provide any other information that may help us locate and improve the issue. If a page cannot be made accessible immediately, we will work to provide the information in another format where practical.

Electronic Format Requests

If you need a specific electronic format, please tell us the format you require, the web page address of the material, and your contact information so we can follow up.

Questions and Feedback

We welcome questions about this accessibility statement and comments on how to improve the website’s accessibility.
