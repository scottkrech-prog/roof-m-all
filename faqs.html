FAQ's · what homeowners ask us

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about roof inspections, repairs, replacements, storm damage, insurance support, cleanup, timelines, and service areas.

Do I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

It depends on the roof age, the amount of damage, and whether the problem is isolated or widespread. Roof-M-All starts with an inspection so you can compare the practical options before deciding.

What are the signs I may need a new roof?

Common warning signs include missing or curled shingles, repeated leaks, storm or hail damage, soft spots, visible wear, or a roof that is nearing the end of its expected life.

Do you offer free roof inspections or estimates?

Yes. You can request a free estimate, and Roof-M-All will follow up to understand the situation and schedule the next step.

Do you help with storm damage and insurance claims?

Yes. If wind or hail may be involved, Roof-M-All can inspect the roof, document visible damage, and help you understand the next steps in the insurance process.

How long does a roof replacement usually take?

Many roof projects can be completed in 1 to 3 days (provided materials are ready and weather cooperates). The exact timeline depends on roof size, complexity, material choice, and any repairs needed.

Can you repair storm damage without replacing the whole roof?

Sometimes. If the damage is isolated, a repair may be the right move. If the damage is widespread or the roof is near the end of its life, replacement may be the better long-term option.

What roofing materials do you work with?

Roof-M-All works with common residential and commercial roofing options including shingles, metal roofing, rubber roofing, flat and low-slope systems, and related exterior components.

Will you protect my property during the job?

Yes. The crew takes steps to protect the property, manage debris, and clean up the work area when the job is complete.

Can I stay in my home during roof replacement?

In most cases, yes. Roofing work happens outside, but it can be noisy. Roof-M-All can explain what to expect before work begins so you can plan around the installation day.

Do you handle gutters and siding too?

Yes. Roof-M-All handles roofing, siding, gutters, storm damage, and commercial roofing, so related exterior needs can be discussed during the estimate.

What areas do you serve?

Roof-M-All serves Charlotte, Mint Hill, Matthews, Weddington, Waxhaw, Marvin, and surrounding North Carolina and South Carolina communities.

What happens after I request an estimate?

Roof-M-All follows up, learns what is going on with the roof or exterior, schedules the appropriate inspection or estimate, and explains the recommended next step clearly.
