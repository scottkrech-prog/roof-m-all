Roof-M-All takes the security and privacy of your personal information seriously. We do not trade, sell, or rent your personally identifiable information. What Information We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry and provide the services you request. This may include your name, phone number, email address, city or ZIP code, address, service interest, project notes, and related roofing or appointment details.

We may also collect anonymous or technical information automatically, including cookies, IP address, browser/device information, page URL, referrer, and campaign or attribution data used to improve website performance and marketing measurement.

In compliance with applicable federal and state laws, Roof-M-All may record or monitor phone calls, text messages, and in-person or virtual appointments for quality assurance, training, customer service, compliance, and verification of communication accuracy.

By communicating with Roof-M-All by phone, SMS, website form, or appointment, you consent to such recording and monitoring where permitted by law. Recordings are used for legitimate business purposes and are not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

TCPA Compliance

By providing your contact information, including your phone number, you consent to receive communications from Roof-M-All regarding your inquiry, appointments, estimates, and roofing services by phone call, text message, or email.

  • Message frequency may vary.
  • Standard message and data rates may apply.
  • You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying “STOP.”
  • For assistance, reply “HELP” or contact Roof-M-All directly.

SMS Terms & Conditions

By providing your mobile number, you consent to receive SMS messages from Roof-M-All related to your inquiry, service request, appointment scheduling, reminders, estimates, and project updates. We do not share mobile opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
